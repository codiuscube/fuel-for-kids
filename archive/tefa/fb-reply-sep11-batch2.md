# Facebook post — Sept 11 waitlist drop (community-wide)

*Community post, not a thread reply. Cody's voice. Plain text on purpose — Facebook
won't render markdown.*

---

As expected, new waitlist awards went out this morning. Not sure the exact amount yet —
last time they posted the number the next morning — but from the bands people are
reporting it looks like maybe around 4,000.

The pattern is pretty steady now. Awards go out, families get four weeks to opt in, then
they purge whoever didn't and cut the next round. That's been landing Thursday night /
Friday morning. Aug 27 was a Thursday, today is Friday.

Expect the next update around September 24, when the Aug 27 awards' four-week windows
close. I'd expect another couple thousand.

From here I'd expect slow but constant updates twice a month or so, shrinking each time,
until the unconfirmed money runs dry.

If you got the text today: you have four weeks to opt in. $0 on the account is normal
until the school verifies enrollment.

Quick note on September 15, because it's on a bunch of official pages and it's easy to
misread. That is not when the next waitlist drop happens. TEFA pays for a school year.
If you join after the year has started, they pay you for the part that's left. Confirm
enrollment by Sept 15 (opt in AND the school verifies you in Odyssey, or confirm
homeschool) and this year is still 100%. After that it's 75% of this year. After Jan 15
it's 50%. That's all "prorated" means — a partial year, not a penalty for not having a
school picked yet. The clock is when enrollment is confirmed, not whether a school name
is sitting in the app. If it already says verified, the school-side step is done.

They do not hold the missing piece for you. It was never put in your account. That money
stays in the program. Different from unused money — dollars that did land in your
account that you didn't spend. Those roll to next year if you stay in.

Next year is a new year. The statute only prorates the year you enroll late (SB 2
29.361(d)). If you stay in the program, next year should be a full award again. Nobody
has published a "once 75%, always 75%" rule.

If you got awarded today and you can finish opt-in and school verify by Tuesday, do
that. You still have four weeks to keep the award either way.

(Own math off the public releases plus what this group is reporting, not an official
number. I'll update when they post a count.)
