Hi all, this will be my last update on the waitlist. This is for the new August wave, and how to get your own confirmed waitlist number.

**Context on how the waves are working in August**

The Comptroller announced today that nearly 15,000 additional awards were **issued** to Tier 3 students. Worth flagging the wording: these are awards, not funded seats — parents get four weeks from their award date to opt in and confirm. There are now **118,441 students with active awards**, and about 85,000 of those are actually funded participants. The gap between those two numbers is the fuel for everything below.

https://educationfreedom.texas.gov/newsupdates/nearly-15000-additional-tefa-awards-issued-to-tier-3-students/

The fact sheet also breaks the 118,441 down by tier: **Tier 1 18% / Tier 2 55% / Tier 3 27%**. That's roughly 32,000 Tier 3 awards, and at the lottery Tier 3 had fewer than 30 statewide. So essentially all of it is cascade — the first official read on how far the line has actually traveled.

**For transparency, here are my own numbers (Tier 3):**

💡 **Quick tip: Odyssey support will confirm your range if you ask them.** I submitted a support request and got a specific band back the same day. They were clear that agents can't see individual rankings and only management can confirm ranges — but they will pass one on. Much better than guessing from portal screenshots.

**On timing**

The last wave was 13 days ago. The next one should come from this latest batch shaking out — families who opt out, switch to homeschool, or simply never confirm once school has actually started. That points to later in August, and possibly another wave a couple of weeks after that.

The official rule is four weeks from award date to opt in and confirm, which would put the full shakeout around September 7. I don't think they'll wait the full four weeks to move again, but they might.

**Predictions on the next wave**

- Up to a 50% decline rate on the August awards
- A higher share of families switching to homeschool this late in the year

Both of those work in the same direction, and it's worth understanding why they're different. A family that **switches to homeschool** frees up real money — that funds a seat further down. A family that **declines** frees no money at all, but the offer passes to the next person on the list for free. So declines don't fund anything; they just make each freed seat travel further.

**What this means**

Likely offer reach is around **~51k** on the original waitlist numbers, probably hitting a ceiling around **60k**.

**The honest caveat**

Nobody has published a cascade depth. Odyssey only ever tells you how many families are ahead of *you* — never an absolute rank. So every "we're at X now" number floating around this group, mine included, is an estimate. The gap they give you is real; the depth is inference.

And the one number none of us has is what share of deep Tier 3 families actually opt in when they're finally offered a seat. That's the whole ballgame for anyone still waiting, and we won't know until these 15,000 resolve.

As always this is my own math off the public Comptroller releases plus what support told me directly — not an official projection. The final calls are the Comptroller's. Happy to share sources, and I'll correct anything that turns out to be off.
