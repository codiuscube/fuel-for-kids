## "Why did you choose NBCA?"

We were looking for a school that would partner with us rather than run parallel to
us — where our kids' education and their faith are the same conversation, not two
separate ones. That mattered most to us.

Beyond that: friendship and growth. We want our kids surrounded by peers, mentors,
and elders who will shape their character, and we're praying they find real friends
here. All three are academically capable and we want them somewhere that will
actually stretch them. And after several years of our kids being in three different
situations, having them under one school community — one culture, one calendar — is
something we've wanted for a long time.
