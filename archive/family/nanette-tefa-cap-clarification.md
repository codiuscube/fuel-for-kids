## Notes / context

### Why this is a clarification, not a re-litigation

Her stated reason is not true as stated. The Sep 12 proposal is at the cap, not over it:
$18,000 + $25,222.50 = $43,222.50 = 90.0% of $48,025, with $4,802.50 = 10.0% paid personally.
Per student it also lands on exactly 90.0%.

Her sentence is only correct under a reading where "TEFA funds" in the policy means the *award*
rather than the *amount applied to tuition*. Under that reading:
$18,000 + $31,422 = $49,422 > $43,222.50.

Both readings are available from the text. The policy enumerates "tuition assistance ... including
TEFA funds ... or any other institutional or external tuition support" — "tuition support" points
toward funds actually applied to tuition. But the award-level reading serves the statement's
evident purpose (school aid shouldn't fund a family's savings), which is why she will probably
confirm it.

### Expect her to say yes, the award counts

"You are still free to choose how you use your TEFA funds for eligible expenses" and "how much you
wish to pay privately" both read like someone who understands that holding funds back is possible
and is declining to raise aid to fund it. The likely outcome is confirmation, not reversal.

That is still worth getting in writing. Under the award-level reading, NBCA aid is fixed at
$11,800.50 and every dollar held back in Odyssey costs $1 cash now — which is the input for the
re-enrollment decision, and for whether the Marcum gift is needed next year.
