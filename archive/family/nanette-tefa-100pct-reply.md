If that's not something the school can do, we'll go with 100% to tuition and you can set the plan up as you described. Either way, thank you for walking us through this.

### Why this ask, and why framed as deferral

- Every dollar of remainder = one more dollar of NBCA aid this year. The cap binds, so we can't create a remainder by allocating less in Odyssey; NBCA sizes aid to the award and bills the difference.
- Under NBCA's own policy, rollover counts as awarded funds next year, so NBCA recovers it by sizing aid lower then. Two-year outlay is unchanged. That's the pitch: timing, not loss.
- Value to us: insurance against an aid cut (statement: "not guaranteed from year to year"); optionality if we leave NBCA; cushion if the 2027 appropriation shrinks.
- Cassius's account closes when he graduates/leaves and funds can't move between siblings. If Nanette asks for a per-child split, put Cassius at 100% to tuition and hold the remainder in Dorothy's and Sebastian's accounts. Don't volunteer this.

- Statute: SB 2 §29.365 (no higher charge for participants) is a weak hook against need-based aid; §29.368 (autonomy) shields the school; §29.373(c) confers no property right. Not worth raising.
