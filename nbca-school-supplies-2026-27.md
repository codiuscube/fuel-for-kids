## Spec deviations worth knowing

1. **Scissors are Amazon Basics, not Fiskars.** Sebastian's list names *Fiskars 6"
   (Age 12+)*. Fiskars' "12+" line is actually 7"; their 6" model is rated 8+. No
   single product matches the list as written. The Amazon Basics pair cuts paper
   fine — swap only if the teacher is strict.
2. **Composition books are 80-sheet, not 100 pg.** Four of them, the right count.
   80 sheets is Walmart's standard.
3. **Great Value wipes, not Clorox.** Six canisters, 225 ct each. The MS and HS
   lists just say "disinfecting wipes"; only Sebastian's line names Clorox.
4. **Kleenex is 8 boxes, not 6.** Two 4-packs. Cassius and Dorothy need 3 each, so
   two are spare. Sebastian's list only asks tissues of girls.
5. **Pencils are 2 × 48 ct, not 3 × 30 ct.** 96 total. Sebastian's classroom
   donation line specifically asks for a 30-ct box, so you may want to decant one.
6. **Glue sticks are 6, not 4.** Two 3-count packs; the list asks for 4.
