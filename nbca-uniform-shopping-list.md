## 👟 Shoes, socks & accessories — CONFIRMED from the official dress code

Source: **NBCA 2026-2027 Secondary Campus Student Dress Code**, boys' and girls' pages.
Covers Cassius and Dorothy. Sebastian is Elementary campus — separate document, still needed.

### Shoes — identical rules for boys and girls
- Any **closed-toe** shoe appropriate for school activities
- Must have a **full front AND back** — **slip-ons are not allowed**
- **No Crocs, water shoes, or slippers**
- No messages, writing, or wheels on the shoe

➡️ **Practical buy:** plain lace-up athletic sneakers. No slip-ons, no big printed
slogans. Both kids are in Athletics as a class period, so one sturdy pair each does
double duty for PE and daily wear.

### Socks — identical rules for boys and girls
- **Matching socks in solid white, hunter (green), or black.** No other colors.
- Girls only: capri-length leggings, ankle-length leggings, or tights in **solid white or
  black only**, and **only under skirts**. No other colors, textures, or patterns.

➡️ **Practical buy:** multipacks of solid white and solid black. Skip patterned/logo socks.

### Belts — ✅ NOT REQUIRED
Belts appear **nowhere** in the secondary dress code — not on the boys' page, not on the
girls' page. **Don't buy any.** (Still unconfirmed for Elementary/Sebastian.)

### Hats
Not worn at school except special events like "Hat Day" during Homecoming. Girls'
headbands/bows must be solid white, black, hunter, or TH White Plaid; nothing covering
the ears in class. No head scarves.
